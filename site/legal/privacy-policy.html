Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29.10.2026

Lionscraft Pte. Ltd. (“Lionscraft”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website lionscraft.io and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, address, and company details through Microsoft Bookings when you schedule a meeting. In addition, technical data such as your IP address, browser and device information, and approximate location are collected via cookies and analytics tools when you visit our website (see section 4).

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data to provide and deliver our services, to communicate with you about your bookings and inquiries, to conduct marketing where you have given consent, to improve and optimize our website and services through analytics if you have consented, and to comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Sharing of Data

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with trusted service providers that help us run our website, such as our analytics provider, Google Analytics.

4. Cookies and Tracking

Our website uses analytics cookies from Google Analytics. They help us understand how visitors use our site, for example which pages are read and how people find us, so we can improve it. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not use these cookies to identify you by name. By using our website, you agree to these analytics cookies. You can opt out at any time by blocking or deleting cookies in your browser settings, or with Google's opt-out add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

5. Your Rights

As a user, you have certain rights under the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and other applicable laws. You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you, the right to request corrections or updates to your personal data, the right to request deletion of your personal data where appropriate, and the right to withdraw your consent to the collection, use, or disclosure of your data, including for marketing and analytics cookies. Requests can be made by contacting us at hello@lionscraft.io.

6. Data Storage & Security

We retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required or permitted by law. When no longer needed, we will remove or anonymize your personal data. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard personal data against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or loss, and access is restricted to authorized personnel only. Your personal data may be stored and processed in Singapore or other jurisdictions. Where data is transferred overseas, we ensure that the receiving organization provides a standard of protection comparable to the PDPA.

7. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Users are encouraged to review this Policy periodically.
