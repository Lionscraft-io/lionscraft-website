## 1. Lido: turning validator best practice into an auditable standard

**Client:** Lido DAO · **Sector:** Liquid staking protocol · **Offer:** Validator Operations to ValOS
**Headline number:** $35B secured via node operations standards **[TBC: the date and source of the $35B figure; Lido's staked value moves with the ETH price]**

### Problem
Lido spreads one of the largest pools of staked ETH across many independent node operators. Each operator ran its own security, key custody and incident-response practices, and no shared, auditable bar existed. Nobody could easily answer the question an institution asks before staking: *how do I know this operator is safe?*

### What we did
- Supported the community effort to define operator best practice, from the earlier DUCK initiative through to ValOS, over roughly three years.
- DUCK produced a risk framework, a mitigation and controls library, and a communications toolkit. ValOS builds on that with governance and organisational structures, so the standard can be used in professional audits.
- **[TBC: Lionscraft's specific contribution, e.g. authored the risk framework, ran working groups, coordinated contributors, drafted the controls library. This is the most important gap: the case study is only as strong as this sentence.]**

### Result
- **ValOS went live in August 2026,** funded by Lido DAO through its LEGO grants programme.
- Lido set aside a **$60K grant pool (DUCK FLAP)** that covers up to 50% of assurance-review costs for node operators who adopt it early.
- The standard is aligned with **ISO 27001 and SOC 2** and published open source (valos.global, `lidofinance/valos` on GitHub), so any operator can be assessed against it.
- **[TBC: how many operators have adopted ValOS or completed an assurance review so far]**

**Why it matters for you:** if you stake at institutional scale, ValOS gives your risk committee a recognised standard to hold operators to. We know it from the inside.

## 2. Munich Re: making validator risk insurable

**Client:** Munich Re · **Sector:** Reinsurance · **Offer:** Tokenized Risk with RIZK / Validator Operations
**Headline number:** 1M+ validators unlocked for insurance

### Problem
Staking brings a new kind of loss: slashing, where a validator's mistakes or misconfiguration lead the protocol to destroy part of the stake. To insure it, an underwriter has to understand how validators fail, how likely each failure is, and which operator controls actually reduce the risk. Insurance teams needed that technical grounding before they could price cover with confidence.

### What we did
- Ran Web3-fundamentals and slashing-mechanics workshops for Munich Re's teams, focused on risk management and how validator failures actually happen.
- Supported Web3 product design for the insurance side. **[TBC: what "product design" meant in practice, e.g. underwriting criteria, risk model inputs, operator due-diligence checklist]**
