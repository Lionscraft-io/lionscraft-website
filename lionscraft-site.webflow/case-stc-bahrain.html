Launching an award-winning Web3 business unit

3
industry awards for infrastructure innovation and technology excellence
Client
stc Bahrain
Sector
Telecommunications
Offer
Strategy & Growth · Digital assets
01 — Problem

stc Bahrain wanted to move into Web3 as an operator, running infrastructure and earning from it rather than just reselling connectivity.

That meant creating a new business unit from scratch: choosing which protocols to back, how to partner with them, and how to run validator infrastructure to telco standards.

02 — What we did
  • Designed and implemented the launch framework for stc Bahrain's Web3 Launchpad, a corporate-led programme to support and develop Web3 protocols. It launched in March 2024.
  • Helped shape its partner pathways, including the Pearling Path for new and emerging protocols. TBC: did Lionscraft design the pathways?
  • Built and ran validator operations behind the programme. Public announcements name stc Bahrain as a validator or infrastructure partner for Core Chain, Allora, ZetaChain, Aleph Zero and Avalanche. TBC: which of these Lionscraft sourced or operated
